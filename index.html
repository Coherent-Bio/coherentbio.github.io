What we're building

Complex biology. Limited data. A different way to learn.

01 / WHAT

Clinical prediction for rare diseases

Coherent Bio is developing technology to help drug developers make better clinical predictions from limited patient data in rare and complex diseases.

02 / HOW

Biologically informed quantum kernels

We are developing quantum kernels that encode relationships among biomarkers, molecular pathways, and clinical indicators into how patient similarity is measured.

Our approach

Build biological structure into the representation.

Rare-disease datasets can contain complex, heterogeneous biological information while providing relatively few patients for a specific prediction task. Our approach incorporates biological structure into the representation used to compare patients and make predictions.

01

Represent the patient

Combine relevant clinical measurements, biomarkers, and biological variables.

02

Encode biological structure

Incorporate known relationships among variables into the design of the quantum feature map.

03

Measure patient similarity

Use the resulting quantum kernel to define a richer similarity measure between patients.

04

Validate rigorously

Compare against strong classical and biology-informed baselines on clinically meaningful prediction tasks.
